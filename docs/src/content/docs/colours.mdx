A palette sets every colour: the ground, the text, secondary text and lines,
an accent for focus, selection, the current place and links, and the
green, yellow and red of asides and changed lines. It comes in a dark and a
light scheme, with code themes to match.

## Try a palette

Pick one. The whole site changes, its code included, for this visit, and the
line below is the option that gives it.

Each keeps its published colours where they read. Where one falls short of
the theme's bar for body text, often a light scheme's text or yellow, only
its lightness moves, as far as it must; its hue and chroma stay.

## The theme's own

`hypha`, the default, is the theme's own palette, turned by three numbers: a
**hue** for the near-greys of the ground, text and lines, an **accent** hue,
and a **tint**, how much colour the greys carry, from `0`, grey, to `2`. A
`hue` given alone brings its accent with it, six degrees on:

```js
starlightThemeHypha({ hue: 30 }); // accent 36
starlightThemeHypha({ hue: 160, accent: 20, tint: 1.5 });
```

The numbers turn the theme's own palette alone; a known palette has its own
colours, and the plugin refuses them beside one.

## Light and dark

`scheme` is the scheme a visit starts in: `auto`, the default, follows the
visitor's system, or `dark` or `light`. The header's control lets the
visitor change it, and their choice is kept for the next visit; a site's
default is never stored, so changing it reaches everyone.

```js
starlightThemeHypha({ scheme: "dark" }); // starts dark, the control can change it
starlightThemeHypha({ themeSelect: false }); // follows the system, no control
starlightThemeHypha({ scheme: "dark", themeSelect: false }); // always dark
```

Without the control, a choice stored on an earlier visit is ignored. Code is
coloured for each scheme the site can show, and the browser's `theme-color`
follows the starting scheme.

## Readable at every one

In both schemes, text reads at 7:1 or more on the ground and the surfaces,
secondary text at 4.5:1, and the accent and the tones mark them at 3:1. The
theme's own palette holds more, text at 12:1, at every hue, accent and tint
the options take. The theme's tests check every palette.

The day's accent is set at an even lightness in OKLCH, so a yellow accent is
as dark as a blue one. It needs a browser from 2024 or later.

## Your own colours

To change a role rather than a palette, set its token. A token on `:root`
changes both schemes; under `[data-theme]`, one:

```css title="src/styles/custom.css"
:root[data-theme="light"] {
  --hypha-color-ivory: #fffdf8;
}
```

The roles and their defaults are under [Tokens](/starlight-theme-hypha/tokens/).
